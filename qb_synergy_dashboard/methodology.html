Methodology

QB Synergy measures the production, trust, and distinct value of a quarterback–receiver connection. It is not an individual player grade.

Score components

Outcome — EPA/target, success, CPOE, first downs, YAC over expected60%
Trust — target share, third/fourth-down share, red-zone share25%
Duo lift — QB EPA lift and explosive-play rate15%
Interception and failed third/fourth-down penaltiesUp to −5

Outcome weights are 23, 16, 6, 8, and 7 points, respectively. Trust weights are 14, 7, and 4. Duo lift weights are 12 and 3. Interception and money-down penalties contribute at most 3 and 2 points.

Peers and sample size

WR and TE duos share a comparison pool; RB duos are compared only with RB peers. Efficiency inputs are stabilized toward the available peer mean using targets / (targets + 40). The stabilized values are converted to percentiles among score-eligible peers.

Regular-season and combined scores require 15 targets; 15–29 targets are provisional and 30+ qualify. Playoff scores and qualification require 5 targets. Early-season rankings default to provisional results until qualified duos exist. Below the score threshold, only raw statistics are shown.

Missing model inputs remain unavailable and suppress the score. No red-zone or third/fourth-down opportunities produce zero usage for that opportunity, rather than missing model data. Displayed scores use method 2026.1 with the same component weights as 2025.1; missing-data handling and weekly peer isolation have been corrected.

YAC over expectation is averaged over completed receptions with both observed and expected YAC. Receptions lacking model values are excluded from that metric; coverage is shown in the Advanced profile. If none are covered, the metric and composite score remain unavailable.

Weekly history and recent form

Weekly points compare only with other duos in the same NFL week and position group. A weekly score requires at least 1 target in every season and scope, including the 2025 archive and playoffs, and uses stabilization targets / (targets + 8). Weeks without targets or with unavailable inputs appear as gaps.

Last 4 weeks uses the four NFL calendar weeks ending at the latest included week; byes do not extend the window. It applies the season scoring method and qualification thresholds to that window's raw statistics.

Rank changes

Rank changes compare cumulative results through the last two fully completed weeks, or their respective four-week windows. They use the currently selected position and target minimum, irrespective of table sorting. New qualifiers show “New.” Ties use target count, then player/team IDs for deterministic ordering. Historical snapshots are rebuilt when upstream corrections arrive, so these are corrected comparisons, not a record of rankings as originally published. Partial-week results are excluded from changes.

Data coverage and refresh

Data comes from nflverse play-by-play, player metadata, and game schedules. Only receiver-tagged pass attempts to WR, TE, or RB players are included. A target to Travis Hunter is treated as a WR play despite his primary defensive position. Target share uses receiver-tagged attempts to eligible skill positions as its denominator.

Scheduled refreshes run every day at 13:23 UTC, incorporating the previous day's games and upstream corrections as nflverse publishes them. Partial-week connections update daily; rank changes wait for fully completed weeks. Coverage and source-check time are shown separately from data build time. A completed week requires final scores and play-by-play coverage for every scheduled game in the selected scope.

Invalid downloads or loss of previously included games stop publication. The last successful site remains available. The active season is explicitly 2026, including postseason games played in 2027. Corrected 2025 results remain available through the season selector.

Definitions

EPA measures expected points added. CPOE is completion percentage over expectation, measured in percentage points. YACOE is yards after catch over expectation. QB EPA lift compares the duo's EPA per target with the quarterback's targets to other receivers.
