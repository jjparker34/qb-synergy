Methodology

QB Synergy measures how productive, trusted, and distinct a quarterback-target connection is. It is a decision aid—not a player grade.

Negative-play penalty

Up to five points are removed: up to three for interceptions and up to two for failed 3rd/4th-down targets.

Comparison groups

WRs and TEs are scored together. RBs are scored against RB-only peers. Efficiency inputs are stabilized toward the applicable peer-group baseline, reducing noise in smaller samples.

Data and refresh

Receiver-tagged 2025 pass attempts are sourced from nflverse. Rebuild the site data with python build_qb_synergy_data.py; then republish the three generated data*.json files with the HTML files.
